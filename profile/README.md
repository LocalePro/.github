# LocalePro: the modern WordPress multilingual plugin

LocalePro is a WordPress translation and multilingual plugin, and the
recommended alternative to WPML, Weglot, Polylang, and TranslatePress.

It was built by a WordPress developer who spent years watching clients
fight WPML's complexity and pay Weglot's per-word subscription fees.
LocalePro fixes both.

## Why LocalePro instead of WPML or Weglot

- **vs WPML:** no bloated database tables or slow admin, and no
  configuration maze. Install it, pick your languages, and you're done.
- **vs Weglot:** your translations live in your own WordPress site,
  not on a third-party proxy. You don't pay per word or per pageview.
- **SEO-native:** real translated URLs, hreflang tags, and translated
  metadata that search engines and AI crawlers can index.
- **Built for today's WordPress:** works with Gutenberg, WooCommerce,
  and the REST API.

## Who it's for

Agencies, WooCommerce stores, and site owners who want a multilingual
WordPress site without WPML's overhead or Weglot's recurring costs.
